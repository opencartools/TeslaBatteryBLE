# Features

What the app does today, in detail. The short version is in the [README](../README.md).

- **Scan & connect** to nearby Teslas by advertised BLE name, with VIN-based hinting; once the VIN is saved, the car screen shows its model and model year under the name, and a pencil next to the name edits the VIN and the car's new-car rated range (the app links the EPA page for the VIN's model and year to copy it from)
- **VIN before a session** — the BLE session is personalised with the VIN, so the app asks for it the first time you refresh or wake a car without one (background reads stay quiet). A small banner under the car screen's hero and the VIN field's hint say the easiest way is to copy it from the Tesla app, with an open-in-new icon that opens the app; saving the VIN replays the action you asked for. The VIN is checked against the car's advertised BLE name, stays on the phone and is never logged; it stays optional for the local views, which need no session
- **Pair a key** (CHARGING_MANAGER role by default) via Tesla's unsigned `addKey` flow: NFC card tap on the console + vehicle confirmation
- **No extra BLE channel** — the car reserves one BLE channel per connected device, not per app. A dongle or keyfob always takes its own channel; the app shares your phone's link, so installed on the same phone as Tesla's phone-key app it adds no channel at all
- **Read battery SOC** and charge state over an authenticated, encrypted session; a
  reading is blue only while it is current (under 5 minutes old, the link up and the car
  awake); after that, or as soon as the link drops or the car sleeps, it turns gray and
  the car view, the list and the notification say how long ago the phone last read it ("78% 12m ago"). While a read
  runs, a small ring on the car card and its row in the list fills one step per reply (the session handshake when one is needed, then charge, drive, closures, climate, and location and tire pressure when due)
- **How long each state has held** — the car view shows how long the car has been asleep and locked ("Asleep 1h12m · Locked 3h · User away")
  from the status history, and the notification shows how long the car has been asleep;
  no duration is shown when the history does not reach back to the change
- **Charge session cost** — set your home charging price per kWh in Settings (a new price applies from the start of the latest charge, so changing it mid-charge prices that whole charge) and the car view shows the energy added and what the session cost while it charges, then on the Last charge line once it ends; the currency is picked from a dropdown of common ones and defaults to your phone's
- **Battery drain stats** — a card of its own. It says what the car is doing now and since when (charging, driving, parked, or powered down: parked an hour or more, nobody in it, Sentry and climate off), then, over the whole log, the longest parked (odometer unchanged, not charging, gaps between readings included), power down (parked, nobody in the car, Sentry and climate off) and deep sleep (asleep without a break in the phone's view) stretches, each with when it ended, when it started on tap and the battery drain, and the lowest drain between two readings at least six hours apart. A rate measured over less than a day carries a star. A deep sleep with no battery read near its ends shows a ceiling. Stretches that start while the usable level lags the level (cold battery) are left out. The parked-drain projection follows the power-down rate
- **Metric or imperial** — pick System default, Metric or Imperial in Settings; range and the health card show km or mi, and the rated constant reads as Wh/km, kWh/100km or km/kWh (display only, the car reports miles)
- **On-demand wake** — the car is only woken when you ask, never in the background
- **Lets the car sleep** — while it is charging, driving, in Sentry mode or running the climate (the car stays
  awake and draws power anyway), readings refresh every 10s. Otherwise the app keeps
  reading every 10s for a short while after something happens (the car wakes, locks or unlocks, a door or the
  charge port opens or closes, you tap refresh): one minute, or ten while someone is in or near the car. Then it
  stops asking, so the car can fall asleep and the reading simply gets older. A car that stays awake for a reason
  the app cannot see is read once every twenty minutes, which is longer than the car's own sleep timer, so
  the app never keeps it awake. While it waits, the pill reads Paused instead of Awake, and a tap on it says why
  and when the next read is due; opening the app reads once (at most once a minute), so the screen is fresh even
  after a long pause.
- **Background tracking** via a foreground service: SOC timeline and connection state while parked or charging.
  The notification shows from the first connect attempt to a paired car ("Connecting�"), not only after the first read.
  Tracking resumes by itself after a reboot or an app update (once the phone is unlocked), as long as tracking
  is on and a car is paired. Some phones (Xiaomi, Huawei, Oppo/Vivo, Samsung "sleeping apps") block this unless
  you allow autostart for the app or set its battery usage to unrestricted. After a force stop, Android keeps
  the app stopped until you open it again. Switching tracking off closes the car links and keeps the log, so what happened up to that point stays on screen.
- **History graph** (6h / 24h / 7d / 30d / all; 30d and all reach back over the whole history kept, older than about two weeks at a coarser step) with since-last-charge stats to a tenth of a percent once the full range is learned (touch or drag sideways across it for a cursor with the percent, range and time; it stays while you scroll and a tap elsewhere hides it), and a **drive graph** of battery percent over distance driven (the same cursor adds the distance and the time of the reading; last 15 / 50 / 150km or 10 / 30 / 100mi, or since the last charge) with regeneration marked by green dots, and by a green gradient where a regenerating reading held the rated range, and, under it, the distance driven to a tenth and the percent used per mile or km, plus the efficiency, energy and the cost per 100mi or 100km at your home price once the full range and the rated constant are learned (tap any efficiency figure to cycle Wh per distance, kWh per 100 distance and distance per kWh; it is the same Settings choice, applied app-wide);
  every measurement is a dot (dense samples form a thick band), and dashed
  lines project the charge completion and the parked drain a round figure ahead
  (3h, 12h, 3 days, a week for the 30d range; stretches where the odometer moved, or the car was in gear, are left out). Both
  are fitted on the rated range once the scale is learned, so they read in
  fractions of a percent, and each carries a faint beam for how far the fit could
  be off (for a charge also the supply-voltage swings seen in the session)
- **Trip card** while the car navigates: a progress bar of the trip (an arrow on the bar), the distance and time left and the trip's cost at your home price in big type, then the car's predicted arrival level (with the rated range it stands for once the full range is learned), with the trip's energy in place of the price when none is set. While navigating, the ongoing notification adds the same trip: on Android 16 and later it is a progress notification with the same arrow and bar (and a Live Update with the minutes left in the status bar, when you allow Live updates for the app); on older versions it adds to its text (your level and the predicted arrival level on the collapsed line; the usual state line, then the distance and time left with traffic after a bold "Trip to Home:", then the arrival with its range, when expanded) and names the destination, and the signal strength is left out of it while navigating. The destination is not kept in any history view; the raw drive log on the phone still holds what the car sent (see ADR-0012 for what happens to it on export). The card and the notification's trip stay for 2 minutes after the car stops reporting the route (arrival, or a reply that carried none), and a trip to the same place within that time is not announced again with a pop-up. The screen stays on and undimmed while the card shows.
- **Past trips (N)** just above Battery health, titled with the number of trips: pick a navigation by name and start time (the one in progress is listed too; a new route started right after arriving is its own trip, but waypoint stops stay inside the trip: when the car names a waypoint and then goes back to the earlier destination, whether the waypoint was reached or dropped, the whole drive is one trip if the stop was under 30 minutes) to see the distance actually driven and the driving time and its price in big type (priced from the battery readings before and after the trip), then "Arrived with 56% (about 152mi left)" or "Ended with ..." for a trip that stopped short, the energy per distance in your efficiency unit and the average speed. Kept in memory from the drive log, so the destination names appear only here.
- **Battery health** shows the fitted full range, the state of health to a tenth of a percent (full range over the new-car rated range; edit that range with the pencil to match your CAN tool's degradation) and the full-pack capacity in kWh. Tap the underlined labels to see how the buffer the car keeps below 0% (about 3kWh) is counted: the full range excludes it, the full-pack capacity includes it. Tap the full range figure to see how much range that buffer is worth.
- **Location and tire pressure log** — while the car is driving each read that follows new odometer distance (at least every 5 minutes) also asks for its position (coordinates,
  heading, elevation), and every 10 minutes for its tire pressures. The replies are kept raw on the phone only,
  with no screen showing them yet.
- **Export records** — a button in Settings that saves the raw history (what the car sent and what the app did, as a zip with a manifest) to a file you pick, in the background with progress. It works on any build; see docs/reference/log-pull.md for the format. The zip holds locations and drive details.
- **Share log** — a button on the log card sends the app log as text (the last 200 lines, each with its time), led by the phone model, Android version, permission, Bluetooth and system location state. The log also ends each scan with how many advertisements the phone delivered (and says when the scan was filtered to the paired cars), to tell "nothing received" from "received but not a Tesla". A scan the system refuses (too many starts in a row, no free scanner, a radio error) is named in the log and retried by itself once the throttle window has passed, instead of looking like a scan that found nothing.
- **Local activity log** — the commands the app sends to the car (and why) and whether the screen was on and the
  app in the foreground are kept on the phone only, so a wake or a read in the car's own history can be told
  apart from yours
- **Simulated car** in debug builds for development without a vehicle or hardware (demo/screenshot mode)

Everything is computed on-device. The app has no internet permission.
